# Scholarship Opportunities — Muhammad Ismail

A public, read-only mirror of my funded-master's scholarship tracker: tracked
opportunities with deadlines, freshly discovered leads, reference documents
(booklets, guides, templates) and my application checklist.

- **Live site:** https://engr-energy.github.io/scholarship-opportunities/
- **Owner:** Muhammad Ismail — BS Energy Engineering, UET Peshawar (graduating July 2027), targeting funded master's entry September 2027.
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-09 (see the "Last updated" badge on the site; data also in `data.json`).

## How the daily update works

Every morning (~07:16 Asia/Karachi) a scheduled discovery run searches for newly
opened fully funded master's, RA/TA and fellowship opportunities and adds them
to my private Scholarship Opportunities space. Shortly after (~07:45), a daily
sync regenerates `index.html` and `data.json` from the space and pushes the
changes here. Nothing on this site is hand-typed: every deadline, link and
document comes from that space. Where a deadline or link is missing it is shown
as "to be confirmed" — never guessed. Always verify on the official programme
page before applying.

## What is here

- `index.html` — the site (tracked opportunities, discoveries, documents, checklist)
- `data.json` — the same data in machine-readable form
- `docs/` — the 19 reference documents (PDF, DOCX, DOC, TXT)

## What is NOT here

Draft grading (the Review tab: professor emails, SOPs and motivation letters
graded against funded-scholarship standards) lives in my private Muse space
only and is not published here.
